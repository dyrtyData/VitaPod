# VitaPod demo scope

VitaPod is a synthetic-data technical prototype. It does not assess medical eligibility,
authenticate a laboratory result, enroll a participant, or send health data to a research
sponsor. Do not use it with real health records.

The demonstration policy is a fixed, illustrative computation over three fictional fields.
It is not a study protocol, medical guidance, a trained model, or a recommendation about any
person. The browser preview is local and in-memory only. The proof pipeline uses a private
synthetic witness; the on-chain registry receives verifier arguments and emits only digests
and the submitting wallet address.

VitaPod does not establish who created an input, whether it originated from a laboratory,
whether a wallet represents a unique person, whether someone is enrolled in a trial, or
whether any workflow is compliant with HIPAA, FDA requirements, or other regulation.
